# Install sequence

Generic order. Adjust per machine family. Do not paste vendor credentials here.

1. Confirm survey notes still match the site.
2. Protect the floor and the customer path.
3. Uncrate. Inventory the kit against what shipped.
4. Place and level. Do not bolt until it sits right.
5. Anchor if the spec or the landlord requires it.
6. Power last, after data and mechanical are set.
7. Bring the unit up. Walk the basic functions you are allowed to test.
8. Photo the finished install for the *private* job folder, not this repo.
9. Haul packaging out. Leave the site cleaner than you found it.
